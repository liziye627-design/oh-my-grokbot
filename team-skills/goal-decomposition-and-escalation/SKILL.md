---
name: goal-decomposition-and-escalation
description: >-
  Use when an operator hands over a goal to a multi-bot team: split it into
  owned workstreams, route each to the right role, and surface only the
  decisions the operator must make.
---
# Steps
1. Restate the goal in one sentence with a concrete done-condition and deadline; if either is missing and guessing would change the result, ask the operator one question.
2. Check the team roster and pick the fewest roles that cover the work (planner, producers, independent reviewer, knowledge keeper).
3. Hand the confirmed goal to the planner to write steps with owner, inputs, artifact, and acceptance criteria.
4. Dispatch each step to its owner with those fields; keep producer and reviewer separate.
5. Route every artifact to the independent reviewer; returned items go back to the owner with the reviewer's reasons.
6. Escalate to the operator only: irreversible or external actions (sending, publishing, paying, deleting), scope or budget changes, conflicting reviewer verdicts, and blockers needing their sign-in or knowledge. Present each as one question with real options.
7. After acceptance, send accepted material to the knowledge keeper and give the operator a short summary: what shipped, evidence location, open gaps.
